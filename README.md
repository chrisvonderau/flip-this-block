# Flip This Block

A short co-op renovation rush. Grab a tool, fix the rooms, and rent them out before the clock runs down.

Open [the live game](https://chrisvonderau.github.io/flip-this-block/) or this folder's `index.html` in a browser. The file is self-contained and works offline.

## Play

- Co-op: two people share one building.
- Versus: each person flips their own building. Most rent wins.
- Solo: flip the block alone, or add a computer helper or rival on Easy, Medium, or Hard.
- There are ten coastal office buildings. Finished rooms pay rent. Between buildings, spend cash on faster boots, a bigger roller, and quicker tools.
- Later buildings add surprise leaks and a picky tenant inspector.

## Controls

- Xbox controller: left stick to move, A to grab or drop, X to hold and work, Start to pause. You can plug a pad in after the game is already open. Two pads work in co-op and versus.
- Keyboard: WASD, Space grab, and F work for the first person. Arrow keys, Enter grab, and Right Shift work for the second. Esc pauses.
- Touch: on-screen stick and buttons. On a wide tablet in versus, each person uses one half of the screen.

Made for a landscape iPhone, iPad, or MacBook, and readable when mirrored to a TV. Sound starts after the first tap or key.
