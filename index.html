Flip This Block

A cheerful renovation rush. Grab a tool, fix the rooms, and rent them out before the clock runs down.

Xbox controller: stick move, A grab or drop, X hold to work, Start pause. You can plug a pad in after the game is open. Two pads work in co-op and versus.
Keyboard: P1 WASD, Space grab, F work. P2 arrows, Enter grab, Right Shift work. Esc pauses.
Touch: on-screen stick and buttons. On a wide tablet in versus, each person uses one half of the screen.

No controller connected. Keyboard and touch work too.

How do you want to play?

Same tools and rooms in every mode. The clock is always ticking.

Solo setup

Add a computer helper or a rival, or flip the block on your own.

Player 1 uses the first controller or WASD. Player 2 uses the second controller or the arrow keys. On a tablet, versus splits the screen into two touch zones.
